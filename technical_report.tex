\begin{abstract}
Open-ended long-video QA requires answers to remain consistent across a film's
characters, relationships, events, and ending state. We treat the movie as the
inference unit: a single request combines timestamped subtitles, chronological
visual observations, and the complete question set to generate one answer bank.
The final SLoMO submission ranked first with 66.36\% accuracy and a 3.02 score.
\keywords{long-video QA \and movie-level inference \and open-ended QA}
\end{abstract}

\section{System Overview}

MovieQA and TVQA established story-centered video QA with movie narratives,
dialogue, and visual evidence~\cite{tapaswi2016movieqa,lei2018tvqa}.
LongVideoBench and EgoSchema extend evaluation to long-horizon video-language
understanding~\cite{wu2024longvideobench,mangalam2023egoschema}. Video-ChatGPT
and Video-LLaMA demonstrate instruction-tuned video-language models
~\cite{maaz2024videochatgpt,zhang2023videollama}. MovieChat and TimeChat
introduce long-video memory and time-sensitive representations
~\cite{song2024moviechat,ren2024timechat}. Qwen-VL provides a versatile
vision-language baseline~\cite{bai2023qwenvl}.
The SF20K Competition Summary identifies information selection and narrative
reasoning as central determinants of story-level QA~\cite{ghermi2026sf20k}.
Our system treats each film as one context unit and produces one coherent
answer bank for its complete question set. Timestamped ASR, chronological
visual observations, and all same-movie questions are assembled into one joint
request; the answerer returns a question-ID keyed JSON bank for conversion to
the submission CSV.

\section{Inference Protocol}

For movie $m$, shared evidence $E_m$ and the complete question set $Q_m$ map to
one bank $\{a_{m,1},\ldots,a_{m,n_m}\}$ in original question order. Audio is
transcribed with faster-whisper large-v3-turbo into timestamped VTT cues. We
retain cue start times and the first and last 40,000 characters when needed.
For a video with $N$ frames, the $i$-th observation is
$\min(N-1,\lfloor(i+0.5)N/16\rfloor)$ for $i\in\{0,\ldots,15\}$, then resized
to a 512-pixel maximum side and JPEG encoded at quality 82. GPT-5.6-Luna
receives the transcript, all ordered frames, and all movie questions in one
zero-shot request. It silently builds a fact ledger and timeline before
returning concise JSON answers with temperature 0, high reasoning effort, and a
5,000-token budget.
